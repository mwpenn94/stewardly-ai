## 2 · Top 5 Channels per Segment (recommended priority)

### Residential Client
1. Existing client referrals (highest LTV, lowest CAC)
2. County parcel + tiered enrichment + outbound (your current backbone)
3. SEC Form 4 + probate + refi triggers (small volume, very high propensity — wealth-event timing)
4. Local chambers + faith communities + BNI (relational compound)
5. Workshops (closing engine for top-decile)

### Commercial Client
1. AZ Corp Commission + NAICS-filtered outbound
2. Existing client + COI referrals (CPA/Atty partners refer here)
3. Industry associations (Cattle Growers, Mining, Tech Council)
4. LinkedIn Sales Nav (titled-targeted: Owner, President, CFO)
5. Workshops + CE events (own brand-build)

### Experienced Pro (recruit)
1. FINRA BrokerCheck + LinkedIn Sales Nav cross-reference
2. AZ DOI licensee roster (newly licensed = movability signal)
3. LinkedIn outbound via Dripify (already in your stack)
4. Estate Planning Council / NAIFA chapter events
5. Industry-specific recruiting platforms (Reverse Mortgage Daily, etc.)

### New Associate (recruit)
1. LinkedIn Jobs + Indeed via Workable (inbound — already running)
2. Davis-Monthan AFB + Fort Huachuca TAP programs (extraordinarily high quality, low cost)
3. UA Eller / ASU career services (target finance grads + adjacent disciplines)
4. LinkedIn outbound to teachers/coaches/officers in transition
5. Veteran organizations (DAV, VFW, AmVets) in AZ

### CPA / Attorney Strategic Partner
1. AZ State Bar + AZ Society of CPAs directories (firm-segmented)
2. Estate Planning Council of Tucson (relational hub)
3. CE-credit-bearing workshops (you provide CE → they attend → you build relationship)

### HR Director
1. AZ employer registry filtered to 50+ employees (workers' comp data)

## 3 · AZ-Specific High-Value Channels (Detail)

### Davis-Monthan AFB — Tucson, AZ (Pima County)
**Why it matters for WealthBridge:** Major USAF base IN your home territory (T1). ~7,000 active-duty + ~4,500 civilian + transitioning service members. The TAP (Transition Assistance Program) preps separating service members — they are *required* to attend a 5-day workshop. WealthBridge can offer a financial planning module, becoming a known advisor/recruiter to the cohort.

**Approach:** Contact base TAP coordinator (currently part of Airman & Family Readiness). Propose a free "Financial Transition for Separating Service Members" 60-min module. ~30-50 separating per month → 360-600/yr seat exposure.

**Segment fit:** New Associate (career-changing veterans), Residential Client (newly-relocating retirees with pension lump-sum decisions), HR Director (post-service civilian roles).

### Fort Huachuca — Sierra Vista, AZ (Cochise County, T2)
**Why:** Army intelligence base. ~6,500 active-duty, ~7,000 civilians. Same TAP structure. T2 county but driveable from Tucson office in ~75 min.

**Approach:** Same as Davis-Monthan. Coordinate via Soldier for Life-TAP Sierra Vista office.

**Segment fit:** Same as Davis-Monthan. Particularly strong for veteran New Associate recruits given the analytical nature of intelligence training.

### AZ Cattle Growers Association
**Why:** Ranching = land-rich, cash-poor, succession-heavy. Classic WealthBridge advanced planning fit. Concentrated in Cochise, Santa Cruz, Graham counties — your T1/T2.

**Approach:** Sponsor regional meeting ($1-3K), present 30-min on "Ranch Succession & Premium-Financed Life Insurance for Land Transfer." Member directory not public — must build relationship via sponsorship to access.

**Segment fit:** Commercial Client (high-LTV).

### AZ Society of CPAs (ASCPA)
**Why:** ~5,000 members. Largest trade body for CPAs in state. Annual Symposium in Phoenix (June) draws ~1,000 — sponsorship + booth + CE presentation slot is the playbook.

**Approach:** Annual sponsorship $5-15K depending on tier. Speak on tax-advantaged life insurance, premium financing, advanced charitable planning. Capture leads via QR-code event sheet → GHL.

**Segment fit:** CPA Strategic Partner (the entire pipeline).

### AZ State Bar Estate Planning Section
**Why:** Subset of attorneys focused on estate, trust, business succession — exact target for partnership.

**Approach:** Section CE meetings in Tucson + Phoenix. Sponsor 1-2/yr, present advanced planning topics co-branded with NLG Advanced Markets. Sponsorship $1-5K.

**Segment fit:** CPA/Attorney Strategic Partner.

### Estate Planning Council of Tucson
**Why:** Multi-disciplinary (CPAs, attorneys, advisors, trust officers). Monthly luncheons. Mike already plausibly attends — convert from attendee to presenter.

**Approach:** Apply to be a board member or program chair. Free relationship-building venue.

**Segment fit:** CPA/Attorney Partner, Commercial Client (peer attendees often refer).

### Sahuarita / Green Valley
**Why:** Retiree-heavy communities south of Tucson. Above-median net worth (Green Valley median age 73, median HH income $54k but median net worth far higher due to home equity + retirement assets). Strong workshop venue.

**Approach:** Free "Sequence of Returns Risk" + "Living Benefits" workshops at retirement community clubhouses (no venue cost — they want speakers). Partner with retirement communities (Rancho Sahuarita, Madera Highlands).

**Segment fit:** Residential Client (retiree-tilted).

### Hispanic Chamber of Arizona (Tucson/Phoenix/Nogales chapters)
**Why:** Strong in Pima + Santa Cruz counties (Nogales border city, ~95% Hispanic). Often-overlooked HNW segment with multi-generational business ownership.

**Approach:** Annual sponsorship + Spanish-language content (compliance-reviewed). Mike's church relationships may overlap.

**Segment fit:** Commercial Client, Residential Client, Nonprofit.

## 4 · Wealth-Trigger Event Sources (Highest Propensity per Lead)

These are small-volume, high-conversion sources. Each lead is a person/entity experiencing a near-term financial planning need.

| Source | Trigger | Volume (AZ) | Lead-to-Conv | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEC Form 4 (insider sales ≥$1M) | Liquidity event | ~5-15/mo | 8-15% | Public-company execs, very HNW |
| County recorder (refi ≥$500K) | Cash-out event | ~30-80/mo | 3-8% | Implies equity availability |
| Probate filings (estates ≥$1M) | Inheritance | ~20-40/mo | 5-12% | Sensitive — relationship approach |
| Divorce filings (high-asset) | Restructure | ~15-30/mo | 2-5% | Sensitive timing |
| Business sale filings (AZ Corp Comm) | Exit liquidity | ~10-25/mo | 10-20% | Highest-LTV residential segment |
| MLS sales ≥$1M | Real estate liquidity | ~40-100/mo | 2-6% | Either buy-side or sell-side relevance |
| FAA aircraft new registration | Wealth signal | ~5-15/mo | n/a — enrichment | Not a lead source per se, but a join key |

**Implementation pattern:** monitor weekly, score against existing prospect database (does this trigger correspond to someone we already had?), and trigger an outreach workflow with trigger-specific messaging.

- County parcel + AZ Corp Commission + NM SOS + WA DOR (volume foundation)
- LinkedIn Sales Nav + Dripify (already deployed — extend filters per segment)
- 1 monthly workshop (rotate segment focus)
- Existing-client referral asks systematized

### Phase 2 (months 4-6): Add wealth-trigger feeds
- SEC Form 4 monitor
- County recorder refi feed
- FAA aircraft registry one-time enrichment join
- Estate Planning Council membership / presenting

### Phase 3 (months 7-12): Add chamber + association sponsorships
- 2-3 chamber memberships in T1
- 1-2 trade association sponsorships (Cattle Growers, ASCPA, AZ Tech Council)
- CE-credit workshop series (CPA/Atty audience)

### Phase 4 (year 2): Expand to NM/WA per same pattern
Once licensing complete (per Compliance Checklist), repeat Phase 1-3 in NM + WA territories, leaning on AZ template.

## 6 · Cost-to-Pipeline Ratio Targets

For monthly tracking. Pipeline = qualified conversations × estimated LTV.

| Segment | Target $ spent : $ pipeline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Residential Client | 1:15 | High volume, lower individual LTV |
| Commercial Client | 1:25 | Lower volume, much higher LTV |
| Experienced Pro | 1:10 | Long sales cycle, but recurring revenue |
| New Associate | 1:8 | Heavier ramp investment needed |
| CPA/Attorney Partner | 1:30 | Compounds — each partner generates ongoing |
| Affiliate | 1:12 | Track A is fastest, B-D longer setup |
| HR Director | 1:20 | Group enrollment lifts ratio |
| Nonprofit Leader | 1:6 | Brand + relational, modest direct LTV |

If monthly ratio falls below half the target sustained for 60 days → halt that channel investment, redirect to higher-performing.
